Who We Serve

Built for the sectors that can't afford downtime

Every industry puts different pressure on a network. Jump to yours below.

Banking & Financial Institutions Educational Institutions Hotels, Resorts & Tourism Hospitals Government Departments Call Centers / BPOs Restaurants & Casinos Brick and Mortar Businesses
Sector 01

Banking & Financial Institutions

Talk to us about Banking

The challenge

Regulatory scrutiny, high-value targets for fraud, and legacy systems that can't simply be switched off for an upgrade.

Our approach

Segmented networks that isolate card and transaction systems, hardened remote access, and audit-ready documentation for every change.

What you get

  • Reduced exposure to fraud and data-theft attempts
  • Network segmentation that limits how far a breach can spread
  • Documentation and logging that supports regulatory review
  • Minimal-downtime upgrade paths for live branches
Sector 02

Educational Institutions

Talk to us about Educational Institutions

The challenge

Hundreds of personal devices on one network, content filtering requirements, and budgets that rarely match the campus size.

Our approach

Segmented student, staff, and guest wireless networks, content filtering, and infrastructure designed to grow a building at a time.

What you get

  • Reliable Wi-Fi across classrooms, labs, and dorms
  • Separate networks for students, staff, and administration
  • Content filtering that meets school policy requirements
  • Phased rollouts that fit annual budget cycles
Sector 03

Hotels, Resorts & Tourism

Talk to us about Hotels

The challenge

Guests expect fast, private Wi-Fi everywhere on the property, while staff and back-office systems need to stay completely separate.

Our approach

Property-wide wireless coverage with guest isolation, PCI-aware network segmentation for payment systems, and integration with access control.

What you get

  • Strong guest Wi-Fi coverage across rooms and common areas
  • Guest traffic fully isolated from staff and payment systems
  • PCI-aware segmentation for point-of-sale and booking systems
  • Access control integration for guest rooms and staff areas
Sector 04

Hospitals

Talk to us about Hospitals

The challenge

Life-critical systems that cannot go down, medical devices with their own quirks, and strict patient-data handling requirements.

Our approach

Redundant network paths for critical systems, segmented device networks, and security auditing aligned to patient-data handling.

What you get

  • Redundant uplinks and failover for life-critical systems
  • Medical devices isolated onto their own segmented network
  • Access control and video security for restricted areas
  • Auditing aligned to patient-data handling expectations
Sector 05

Government Departments

Talk to us about Government Departments

The challenge

Public-facing services alongside sensitive internal systems, procurement processes, and a high bar for accountability.

Our approach

Clearly documented, auditable infrastructure with public and internal systems properly separated, built through a transparent procurement process.

What you get

  • Internal systems separated from public-facing services
  • Auditable configuration and change documentation
  • Vendor-neutral procurement support
  • Security hardening aligned to public-sector expectations
Sector 06

Call Centers / BPOs

Talk to us about Call Centers / BPOs

The challenge

Hundreds of workstations and VoIP lines that all depend on one network staying up, with zero tolerance for downtime during shifts.

Our approach

High-density wired and wireless infrastructure sized for workstation count, with QoS tuned for voice traffic and redundant core switching.

What you get

  • Infrastructure sized for high workstation density
  • QoS configuration tuned to prioritize voice traffic
  • Redundant core switching to avoid shift-wide outages
  • Rapid device procurement for fast-scaling floors
Sector 07

Restaurants & Casinos

Talk to us about Restaurants

The challenge

Point-of-sale systems, guest Wi-Fi, and video security all running on one site, often with no formal network design at all.

Our approach

Segmented POS and guest networks, video surveillance covering cash and gaming areas, and cabling designed for a working floor, not an office.

What you get

  • POS systems isolated from guest and general Wi-Fi
  • Video surveillance covering cash handling and floor areas
  • Reliable guest Wi-Fi without exposing internal systems
  • Cabling and equipment built to survive a working floor
Sector 08

Brick and Mortar Businesses

Talk to us about Brick and Mortar Businesses

The challenge

Day-to-day operations depending on a network that was never properly planned, usually assembled piece by piece over the years.

Our approach

A right-sized infrastructure assessment and upgrade plan that matches your actual operation, without enterprise pricing for a small footprint.

What you get

  • Infrastructure assessments scoped to your actual size
  • Upgrade plans staged to match available budget
  • Device procurement sized to real requirements
  • Ongoing support without an enterprise-sized contract

Don't see your sector listed?

Most of what we design transfers across industries. Tell us what you run and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
